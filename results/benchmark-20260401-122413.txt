  MATHEMATICAL NOTE:

  Ground truth = dot(q, k) in original space (pre-rotation).

  Hadamard rotation is orthogonal (D @ H), preserving dot products:

    dot(q, k) = dot(rotated_q, rotated_k) = dot(rotated_q, quantized_k) + error

  The quantization error = dot(rotated_q, rotated_k - quantized_k)

  Two-term QJL formula: score = MSE_term + QJL_term

    MSE_term  = dot(rotated_q, quantized_k)          (baseline)

    QJL_term  = alpha * dot(rotated_q, H @ D @ signs) (corrects residual)

  QJL is an unbiased estimator of the residual quantization error.

  Result: QJL reduces score-level MSE by correcting quantization bias.

  Note: QJL benefit is BITRATE-DEPENDENT: 4-bit shows best improvement, 2-bit degrades.

  QJL is an UNBIASED estimator — it reduces expected error, not worst-case error.

  At high error (2-bit): injected noise > correction benefit → QJL hurts.

  At low error (4-bit ctx>=4096): helps Standard (+29.6%%) but hurts DeepLayer (-10.9%%).

  Routing: QJL enabled at 4-bit ctx>=4096, disabled otherwise. Aggregate benefit is positive.

  RECOMMENDATION: Enable QJL at 4-bit ctx>=4096, disable at 2-3bit or ctx<4096.
